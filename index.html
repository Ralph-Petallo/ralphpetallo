About Me

A little background on who I am and what I build.

I am a 4th-year BSIT student at Surigao del Norte State University (SNSU), focusing on web-based application development. I work with front-end and back-end technologies to build systems that are fast, easy to use, and useful in real settings.

My capstone project, CSS Prep, is a web-based application for NC II competency assessment in Computer Systems Servicing. It lets students take practice assessments, view their results, and track their progress before the national assessment.

I'm looking for opportunities to build and maintain web applications, keep learning modern frameworks, and grow with a team.
